          How to play
          
  
          You wake up inside a maze. Find the keys, reach the exit door, and escape before the timer hits zero.
          
  
          Keyboard controls
          
  
            
    
          • Up Arrow — move forward
            • 
    
          • Down Arrow — move backward
            • 
    
          • Left Arrow / Right Arrow — turn
            • 
    
          • Enter — interact: take keys and bonuses, flip switches, open doors, disarm a trap ahead, use the exit
            • 
    
          • L — listen: full description of this spot
            • 
    
          • S — status: time, score, lives, keys
            • 
    
          • R — repeat the most recent important clue
            • 
    
          • H — help
            • 
    
          • M — toggle built-in speech narration
            • 
    
          • Escape — pause or resume
            • 
  
          
  
          Listening
          
  
            
    
          • Sounds are positioned left, right, ahead or behind you, following the open path — not through walls.
            • 
    
          • Sounds behind you are muffled and quieter.
            • 
    
          • Every sound also has short text, so headphones help but are never required.
            • 
    
          • At the start you get a clue about what the exit sounds like. Press R to hear it again.
            • 
  
          
  
          Things you will meet
          
  
            
    
          • Keys jingle. You need them to open the exit.
            • 
    
          • Locked doors rattle. A switch somewhere powers them.
            • 
    
          • Switches hum, and may open a hidden passage.
            • 
    
          • Traps hiss faintly when you are next to them. Stepping on one costs a life and ten seconds. Work out which direction the hiss is coming from, turn to face it, and press Enter to disarm it for points.
            • 
    
          • Bonus items chime and raise your score.
            • 
    
          • On harder levels something follows you. Listen for footsteps.
